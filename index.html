FAQ

Need More Information?

Browse our frequently asked questions for quick answers about bookings, services, maintenance, repairs, and installations across Sydney.

How often should I service my air conditioner?
We recommend servicing your air conditioning system at least once a year to keep it running efficiently and reliably. Regular maintenance helps prevent unexpected breakdowns, improves energy efficiency, and extends the lifespan of your system.
Why is regular air conditioning maintenance important?
Regular maintenance ensures your air conditioner operates at its best. It helps improve cooling performance, maintain indoor air quality, reduce energy consumption, and identify potential problems before they become costly repairs.
What does an air conditioning service include?

A standard air conditioning service may include:

  • Cleaning or checking air filters.
  • Inspecting indoor and outdoor units.
  • Cleaning coils and checking drainage.
  • Checking refrigerant system performance.
  • Inspecting electrical connections.
  • Testing cooling and heating performance.
  • Checking overall system operation.
How do I know if my air conditioner needs servicing?

Some common warning signs include:

  • Weak airflow or poor cooling.
  • Unusual noises or unpleasant smells.
  • Water leaking from the indoor unit.
  • Higher-than-normal electricity bills.
  • Air conditioner taking longer to cool the room.
  • System frequently switching on and off.

If you notice any of these issues, it's best to arrange a professional inspection.

Why is my air conditioner not cooling properly?
There are several possible reasons, including dirty air filters, blocked coils, low refrigerant levels due to a leak, drainage issues, or mechanical faults. A professional technician can inspect your system and identify the cause.
How often should I clean my air conditioning filters?
For most residential systems, we recommend checking filters every month and cleaning them every 1–3 months, depending on usage, pets, dust, and environmental conditions. Clean filters help maintain good airflow and cooling efficiency.
How long does an air conditioning service take?
A standard maintenance service typically takes around 45–90 minutes, depending on the type, condition, and accessibility of the air conditioning system. More complex systems may require additional time.
Can regular maintenance reduce my electricity bills?
Yes. A properly maintained air conditioner can operate more efficiently, helping reduce unnecessary energy consumption. Clean filters, clear airflow, and well-maintained components allow your system to work more effectively.
Do you service all types of air conditioning systems?

Professional air conditioning technicians can service a range of systems, including:

  • Split system air conditioners.
  • Multi-split systems.
  • Ducted air conditioning systems.
  • Commercial air conditioning systems.
  • Reverse-cycle air conditioners.

Please contact us to confirm service availability for your particular system.

What should I do if my air conditioner is leaking water?
Turn off the air conditioner and arrange an inspection. Water leakage may be caused by a blocked drain line, a damaged drain tray, or other system issues. Prompt attention can help prevent water damage.
Is air conditioning maintenance necessary even if my unit is working properly?
Absolutely! Even if your air conditioner appears to be working normally, internal components can accumulate dust and dirt over time. Preventative maintenance helps identify hidden issues and keeps your system operating reliably.
When is the best time to book air conditioning maintenance?
The best time is before the peak summer or winter season, when your system is likely to be used more frequently. Booking ahead helps ensure your air conditioner is ready when you need it most.
Do you provide air conditioning repairs as well as maintenance?
Yes, air conditioning repairs may be available for systems experiencing cooling issues, unusual noises, water leaks, electrical faults, or other operational problems. Our technicians can assess the issue and recommend the appropriate solution.
How can I book an air conditioning service?
Booking is easy! Simply contact our team by phone or through our online enquiry form. We'll help arrange a convenient appointment to keep your air conditioning system running smoothly.
